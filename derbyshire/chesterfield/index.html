ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Chesterfield school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Chesterfield depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

More about the service in Chesterfield

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Chesterfield

A music service is the whole thing, not a teacher: staffing, cover when somebody is ill, instruments, records of who played what, and one invoice. That is what we run in Chesterfield.

In Chesterfield that covers 63 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

In-school music service in Chesterfield

Nothing happens after the bell. A child in Chesterfield walks to the room, has their lesson and walks back, and the only thing that changes on your timetable is that a room is booked.

Children in Chesterfield learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

School music lessons in Chesterfield

Lessons one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, during the school day, with a practice note going home after each one. That is the shape of it in Chesterfield.

What a Chesterfield school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Peripatetic music teachers in Chesterfield

The word is old and the arrangement is simple: the same teacher comes to your Chesterfield school every week, knows the children by name, and belongs to a round rather than to a booking.

When a teacher on the Chesterfield round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

Instrumental tuition in Chesterfield

A child who chose their instrument practises. A child who was handed one usually does not. So tuition in Chesterfield starts from what the child asked for, taught by somebody whose own instrument it is.

A lesson in Chesterfield is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

Music lessons for Chesterfield schools

Provision usually fails on administration rather than on teaching. A Chesterfield school gives us a room and a slot; we hold the staffing, the cover, the safeguarding evidence and the billing, and hand the registers back.

The 63 primary schools listed for Chesterfield come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

School band and ensemble in Chesterfield

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In Chesterfield that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.

Chesterfield sits on the same weekly round as Derby, Swadlincote and Ilkeston, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.
