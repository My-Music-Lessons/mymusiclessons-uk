ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Carlisle school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Carlisle depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Carlisle

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Carlisle

What a school is actually buying is the absence of a problem: no cover to find, no instruments to insure, no safeguarding file to keep, no invoices to chase. In Carlisle that is the whole of our side of it.

What a Carlisle school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

In-school music service in Carlisle

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Carlisle.

Carlisle is in Cumbria, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

School music lessons in Carlisle

Lessons one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, during the school day, with a practice note going home after each one. That is the shape of it in Carlisle.

The 45 primary schools listed for Carlisle come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

Peripatetic music teachers in Carlisle

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Carlisle has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

A lesson in Carlisle is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

Instrumental tuition in Carlisle

A child who chose their instrument practises. A child who was handed one usually does not. So tuition in Carlisle starts from what the child asked for, taught by somebody whose own instrument it is.

Carlisle sits on the same weekly round as Barrow-in-Furness, Kendal and Whitehaven, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

Music lessons for Carlisle schools

For a school this is provision without administration: we staff it, cover it, record it and bill the families directly. Carlisle schools keep their timetable and lose the paperwork.

Children in Carlisle learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

School band and ensemble in Carlisle

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In Carlisle that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.

When a teacher on the Carlisle round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.
