WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what schools in Worcester provide

If your school needs more lesson time, we can discuss the options. The current agreement remains in place until a new arrangement is agreed.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Instruments are supplied at no cost to the school, so you can introduce lessons without buying equipment first.
Checks and training
Every teacher has an enhanced DBS check, identity and reference checks, and safeguarding training. Schools can request the supporting records.
A page for your school
Your school has its own lesson page showing its name, address and available instruments. Parents use that page to apply.
The running of it
Day-to-day lesson administration is managed by our team. Your school has a named contact for questions or issues that need attention.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
Provide a suitable room and agree a rotating lesson time with us to help children avoid repeatedly missing the same subject.
One person to ask
Choose a school contact who can help with practical questions about rooms, dates and timetable changes.

Schools in Worcester can enquire about Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele. We provide lesson instruments at no cost to the school; available teaching options are confirmed during the enquiry.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Worcester depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Prices chosen by the school

    Each school chooses its lesson price. After teacher pay, programme operating costs and job-advertising costs, MML pays 50% of the remaining lesson income directly into the school’s account each month. See school prices and monthly payouts ↗.

  • Family lesson payments

    Parents contract with My Music Lessons and pay us directly at the price their school chooses. See pricing and monthly payouts ↗.
