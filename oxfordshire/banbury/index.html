ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Banbury school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Banbury depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

Peripatetic music teachers in Banbury

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Banbury has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

Instrumental tuition in Banbury

A child who chose their instrument practises. A child who was handed one usually does not. So tuition in Banbury starts from what the child asked for, taught by somebody whose own instrument it is.

A lesson in Banbury is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

Music lessons for Banbury schools

Provision usually fails on administration rather than on teaching. A Banbury school gives us a room and a slot; we hold the staffing, the cover, the safeguarding evidence and the billing, and hand the registers back.

Children in Banbury learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

School band and ensemble in Banbury

Up to five children, one room, one piece of music. A band or a small ensemble is where a child stops practising and starts playing with other people, which is the part a lesson on its own cannot give them - and in Banbury it happens inside the school day like everything else we run.

When a teacher on the Banbury round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.
