              TAKE THE PLAN WITH YOU

              A useful copy, wherever you plan.

              Download a calendar snapshot or spreadsheet-friendly CSV. Choose Print pack in the view menu for a separate page for each room, staff role or group.

              Calendar downloads are snapshots, not calendar sync or invitations. Import the .ics file into your calendar manually; later diary changes need a fresh download and careful replacement of the old copy. Dates and times use Europe/London.

              Using the diary well

              Free to use. No subscription or account needed. An optional school email account saves one private programme so you can return and edit it. This is an in-school programme diary with no billing, portals or live AI.

              Start with your school’s term dates. Add rooms and staff role labels if you want to check for double bookings. Add closures before setting up repeating sessions.

              The diary flags overlapping sessions that share an assigned room, staff role or group label. It cannot check supervision, room suitability, travel time or equipment availability. Review the programme with your school before using it.

              This free tool plans your school programme. It does not book My Music Lessons access, allocate pupil accounts or connect to a live AI teacher. Keep personal and pupil information out of every label and downloaded file.
