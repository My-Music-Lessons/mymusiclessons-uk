Telford: a music service inside the school day

We staff it, cover it and record it. Your school keeps its timetable.

We work with Telford schools so that learning an instrument happens at school, like everything else a child learns.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Telford school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

It runs without you week to week, and there is a named person here for when it should not.

A room and a slot

One person to ask

In Telford that is the same arrangement in every school we work with, across all 50 of the primaries listed on this page.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Telford depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Telford

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Telford

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Telford schools.

Children in Telford learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

In-school music service in Telford

Nothing happens after the bell. A child in Telford walks to the room, has their lesson and walks back, and the only thing that changes on your timetable is that a room is booked.

Telford is in Shropshire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

School music lessons in Telford

A lesson in Telford is one child, or two or three learning the same thing alongside each other, or up to five playing one piece together. Which of those a child gets depends on the child, not on what is cheapest to run.

What a Telford school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Peripatetic music teachers in Telford

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Telford has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

When a teacher on the Telford round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

Instrumental tuition in Telford

Tuition on the instrument the child actually wants, taught by somebody who plays it, inside the Telford school day.

In Telford that covers 50 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Music lessons for Telford schools

For a school this is provision without administration: we staff it, cover it, record it and bill the families directly. Telford schools keep their timetable and lose the paperwork.

A lesson in Telford is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

School band and ensemble in Telford

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In Telford that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.

The 50 primary schools listed for Telford come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.
