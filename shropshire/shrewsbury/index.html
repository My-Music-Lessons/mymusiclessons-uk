ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Shrewsbury school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Shrewsbury depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

In-school music service in Shrewsbury

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Shrewsbury.

Shrewsbury is in Shropshire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

School music lessons in Shrewsbury

Peripatetic music teachers in Shrewsbury

Music lessons for Shrewsbury schools

What we ask of a Shrewsbury school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.

School band and ensemble in Shrewsbury

Up to five children, one room, one piece of music. A band or a small ensemble is where a child stops practising and starts playing with other people, which is the part a lesson on its own cannot give them - and in Shrewsbury it happens inside the school day like everything else we run.
