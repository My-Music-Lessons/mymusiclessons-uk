WHAT WE HOLD

Privacy

These pages collect nothing until you send us something, and this says exactly what happens when you do.

READING THE SITE

Nothing, while you are reading

There is no account to make, nothing to log in to, and no cookie set by us on any page of this site. There is no analytics script, no advertising tag and no third-party code of any kind - which is why you have not been asked to agree to anything. The pages are plain files. Reading one leaves nothing behind with us.

The company that serves these files keeps its own short-lived server records, as any web host does, and we neither see nor use them.

THE ENQUIRY FORM

What you send, and where it goes

The form at the foot of every page asks for the name of a school, a town or postcode, your name, your role, a school email address and, if you want to give it, a phone number and anything else you would like us to know. It also carries the address of the page you were reading, so that we know which one brought you here.

Because these pages have no server behind them, the form posts to a form relay, api.web3forms.com, which turns it into an email and sends it to us. The relay is the only third party any of this touches. We use what you send to reply to you about music lessons at your school and for nothing else: no mailing list, no advertising, and nothing passed to anybody else.

We keep an enquiry while the conversation it started is live and for as long afterwards as our own records require. If you would rather we did not, say so - to the address below - and we will delete it and tell you we have. You can also ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or object to us holding it at all.
