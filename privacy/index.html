PRIVACY

What this website collects.

Nothing.

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, carries no third-party scripts, embeds nothing from another domain and has no form to fill in. There is nothing here that identifies you, because there is nothing here that asks.

IF YOU WRITE TO US

An email is the only thing we receive

If you email support@yourmusichub.co.uk, we hold what you send us: your name, your address, the school you are writing about and whatever you tell us. We use it to answer you and, if a partnership follows, to run it. We do not sell it, we do not use it to advertise to you, and we do not pass it to anybody outside the people who need it to answer you.

If you would rather we deleted an enquiry, write to the same address and ask, and we will.

WHAT IS NOT HERE

The rest of the service is not on this site

Lessons, places, registers and payments are run in our own system, which is not part of this website and is not reachable from it. When a school or a family joins us, they are given the terms that apply to them before anything begins, and those terms say what we hold and why. This page covers this website and nothing else.
