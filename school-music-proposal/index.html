FREE TO USE · MY MUSIC LESSONS

ONE CONNECTED SCHOOL DECISION

Make the case. Show the workings.

Build a discussion paper for your school team. The proposal uses your own musical aim, time and cost assumptions. It does not approve a programme, promise learner outcomes or provide a My Music Lessons quotation.

Choose figures files downloaded from those tools. They are read in this page, never uploaded. Imported assumptions remain editable.

01 / Give the programme a purpose.

Instruments to discuss

02 / Make the weekly time work.

Plan one individual slot per pupil each week. Count only complete usable stations and arrange staff supervision separately. These are your assumptions, not confirmed teaching availability.

03 / Put the full costs on the table.

Leave an unknown estimate blank. Enter zero only when that is your assumption. Use a school-specific teaching quotation. Use a consistent VAT basis; tax is not added.

Include supervision, preparation, records and parent communication when considering staff time. These costs support a discussion; they do not recommend a charge to families.

04 / Agree the decisions still needed.

The school retains its own approval, supervision, accessibility and data-protection arrangements. If you select My Music Lessons, the paper records a service to discuss with the team.

Start with the musical purpose. No account or email address needed.
