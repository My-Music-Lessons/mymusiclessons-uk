THE SHAPE OF IT

What each side brings

We bring

  • Teachers, recruited, checked and paid by us
  • Cover when a teacher cannot come, arranged by us
  • Instruments for the children who need them
  • The timetable, the registers and the paperwork
  • One named person at our end who knows your school

The school brings

  • A room, for the same slot each week
  • A named contact who can settle a timetable question
  • The children, out of lessons your staff agree to

THE FIRST TERM

From an email to a first lesson

  • An email and a reply

    You tell us the school and roughly how many children. We tell you what we could run and when a round reaches you.

  • A short call

    Which instruments, which year groups, which slot in the week, and how places would be paid for at your school.

  • A written agreement

    What we provide, what the school provides, the rate, the notice on either side and how it ends. Nothing starts before it is signed.

  • The first visit

    A teacher, a room and a register. From then on it is a weekly fixture and you hear from us when something changes, not when something has already gone wrong.

PAYING FOR PLACES

Three ways, and the school decides which

The school funds every place. We invoice the school and no family is asked for anything.

The school funds a set number of places and other families pay for theirs. The children in the same group have identical lessons whichever applies, and no family ever sees what the school pays.

Every place is family-paid. Families hold their own arrangement with us and the school carries nothing.

Which of the three applies is a decision for the school and its funding. We will tell you what is possible at yours.
