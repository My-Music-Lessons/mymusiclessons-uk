SETTING UP SCHOOL MUSIC LESSONS

From your first enquiry to the first lesson

  1. 01

    Tell us about your school

    Start with your school name and contact details. We will discuss your existing music provision, the lessons you would like to offer, funding arrangements and a possible start date.

  2. 02

    A room and a rotating slot

    Your school provides a suitable room and agrees lesson times with us. A rotating timetable helps pupils avoid repeatedly missing the same subject. We plan the schedule around the school day together.

  3. 03

    Teaching and cover

    Our teachers deliver individual lessons, small groups and bands of up to five pupils. We manage staffing and cover arrangements when a teacher is absent, keeping your school informed about any changes.

  4. 04

    Lesson records and family updates

    We keep registers, practice notes and teacher safeguarding records, with evidence available to your team on request. Families receive a practice note after each lesson so they know what their child has worked on.

QUESTIONS FROM SCHOOLS

School music partnership questions

Key details to help headteachers and music leads plan music lessons for their school.

Who pays for a place?
Each place is either fully school-funded or paid for by the family at a flat monthly rate. The two arrangements are separate: a school-funded place is free to that family. A school can offer funded places alongside family-paid places, with its funding arrangements agreed before lessons begin.
What does the school need to provide?
Your school provides a suitable teaching room, agreed lesson times and a main contact. We supply lesson instruments at no cost to the school and manage staffing, cover, lesson records and administration. We bill families directly for family-paid places. Any school-funded places are agreed separately.
How many children are in a lesson?
Lessons have between one and five pupils. Options include individual teaching, small groups, and bands or ensembles of up to five. We discuss the format with your school to suit the pupils and the provision you want to offer.
What can they learn?
Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing, Ukulele. We agree the instrument options with your school, taking account of pupil interest and teaching availability.
What about safeguarding?
Every teacher completes enhanced DBS, identity and reference checks and safeguarding training before teaching begins. We hold the evidence and make it available to your school on request. Registers and practice notes provide a record of lessons.
How long does it take to start?
We agree a start date after discussing your timetable, room, funding arrangements and teaching availability. These details determine when lessons can begin, so we confirm the timing with your school during the enquiry process.
