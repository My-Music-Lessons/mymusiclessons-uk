Whole-school music provision in Huntingdon

We staff it, cover it and record it. Your school keeps its timetable.

We run an in-school music service in Huntingdon: our teachers come into the school during the school day, so lessons happen where the children already are.

Counting the ones just outside it, Huntingdon has 29 primary schools. Brampton Village Primary School is the largest, at around 610 children, and 4 of them are the size an in-school music service usually runs in.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Huntingdon school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what a Huntingdon school provides

Provision can grow. A school asks for more time, and the arrangement already in place carries on unchanged until a new one is agreed.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Free to the school, brought by the teacher and taken away again. Nothing to buy, nothing to store.
Checks and training
Every teacher is DBS checked to enhanced level and safeguarding trained before they join a round, not after.
A page for your school
Your own lesson page, with your name, your address and the instruments actually offered. Parents start there.
The running of it
Attendance, billing and family contact are ours. One route for a question, one for a change, neither ends with you.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
One room and one recurring slot in term time. Everything around it is ours to arrange.
One person to ask
A name we can reach when a room changes. It is rarely more than a message.

In Huntingdon that is the same arrangement in every school we work with, across all 29 of the primaries listed on this page.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Huntingdon depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Huntingdon

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Huntingdon

What a school is actually buying is the absence of a problem: no cover to find, no instruments to insure, no safeguarding file to keep, no invoices to chase. In Huntingdon that is the whole of our side of it.

What a Huntingdon school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

In-school music service in Huntingdon

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Huntingdon.

A lesson in Huntingdon is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

School music lessons in Huntingdon

Lessons one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, during the school day, with a practice note going home after each one. That is the shape of it in Huntingdon.

Huntingdon is in Cambridgeshire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Peripatetic music teachers in Huntingdon

Our teachers travel to schools across Huntingdon on a fixed weekly round, which is what makes cover possible when one of them is ill - the school is never left with an empty room.

When a teacher on the Huntingdon round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

Instrumental tuition in Huntingdon

Tuition rather than an activity: there is a thing to learn, a way of telling whether it is being learnt, and a note home saying what to practise before next week. That is what a Huntingdon family is paying for, or what a Huntingdon school is funding on their behalf.

In Huntingdon that covers 29 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Music lessons for Huntingdon schools

What we ask of a Huntingdon school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.

Huntingdon sits on the same weekly round as Cambridge, Peterborough and St Neots, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

School band and ensemble in Huntingdon

Five is the largest number where everybody still has a part worth playing. A band that size in Huntingdon is the point at which a child stops counting bars on their own and starts listening to somebody else.

Children in Huntingdon learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.
