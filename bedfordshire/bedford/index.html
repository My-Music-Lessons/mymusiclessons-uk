ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Bedford school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what a Bedford school provides

The agreement is a starting point rather than a ceiling. Ask for more and the existing one holds while we work out what is possible.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Free to the school, brought by the teacher and taken away again. Nothing to buy, nothing to store.
Checks and training
Every teacher is DBS checked to enhanced level and safeguarding trained before they join a round, not after.
A page for your school
Your own lesson page, with your name, your address and the instruments actually offered. Parents start there.
The running of it
It runs without you week to week, and there is a named person here for when it should not.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
One room and one recurring slot in term time. Everything around it is ours to arrange.
One person to ask
Someone who can answer a question about a room or a date. Most weeks there is nothing to ask.

In Bedford that is the same arrangement in every school we work with, across all 66 of the primaries listed on this page.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Bedford depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Bedford

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Bedford

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Bedford schools.

What a Bedford school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

In-school music service in Bedford

In school means in the building, in the timetable, in term time. Our teachers hold a room in Bedford schools and children come to them between lessons.

Children in Bedford learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

School music lessons in Bedford

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Bedford knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

Bedford sits on the same weekly round as Luton, Dunstable and Leighton Buzzard, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

Peripatetic music teachers in Bedford

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Bedford has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

The 66 primary schools listed for Bedford come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

Instrumental tuition in Bedford

Tuition on the instrument the child actually wants, taught by somebody who plays it, inside the Bedford school day.

In Bedford that covers 66 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Music lessons for Bedford schools

Provision usually fails on administration rather than on teaching. A Bedford school gives us a room and a slot; we hold the staffing, the cover, the safeguarding evidence and the billing, and hand the registers back.

When a teacher on the Bedford round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

School band and ensemble in Bedford

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In Bedford that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.

A lesson in Bedford is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.
