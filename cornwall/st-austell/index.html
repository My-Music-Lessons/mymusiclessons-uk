ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a St Austell school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

What we bring, and what a St Austell school provides

Provision can grow. A school asks for more time, and the arrangement already in place carries on unchanged until a new one is agreed.

Ours to provide

The instruments
A child wanting to try the drums needs no drum kit at home. The instruments cost the school nothing.
Checks and training
Every teacher is DBS checked to enhanced level and safeguarding trained before they join a round, not after.
A page for your school
Parents apply from a page about their own school. Nothing arrives at your reception by mistake.
The running of it
Attendance, billing and family contact are ours. One route for a question, one for a change, neither ends with you.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
A room to teach in and a slot that moves through the week. Nothing else on your timetable changes.
One person to ask
Someone who can answer a question about a room or a date. Most weeks there is nothing to ask.

In St Austell that is the same arrangement in every school we work with, across all 18 of the primaries listed on this page.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in St Austell depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in St Austell

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for St Austell

What a school is actually buying is the absence of a problem: no cover to find, no instruments to insure, no safeguarding file to keep, no invoices to chase. In St Austell that is the whole of our side of it.

A lesson in St Austell is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

In-school music service in St Austell

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in St Austell.

The 18 primary schools listed for St Austell come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

School music lessons in St Austell

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in St Austell knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

St Austell sits on the same weekly round as Truro, Falmouth and Newquay, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

Peripatetic music teachers in St Austell

Our teachers travel to schools across St Austell on a fixed weekly round, which is what makes cover possible when one of them is ill - the school is never left with an empty room.

What a St Austell school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Instrumental tuition in St Austell

Tuition on the instrument the child actually wants, taught by somebody who plays it, inside the St Austell school day.

When a teacher on the St Austell round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

Music lessons for St Austell schools

Provision usually fails on administration rather than on teaching. A St Austell school gives us a room and a slot; we hold the staffing, the cover, the safeguarding evidence and the billing, and hand the registers back.

In St Austell that covers 18 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

School band and ensemble in St Austell

Up to five children, one room, one piece of music. A band or a small ensemble is where a child stops practising and starts playing with other people, which is the part a lesson on its own cannot give them - and in St Austell it happens inside the school day like everything else we run.

St Austell is in Cornwall, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.
