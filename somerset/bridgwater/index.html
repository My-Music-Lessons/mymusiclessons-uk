WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what schools in Bridgwater provide

Schools can ask to expand their music provision. We assess requests for more time while keeping the agreed lessons in place.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Instruments are supplied at no cost to the school, so you can introduce lessons without buying equipment first.
Checks and training
Every teacher has an enhanced DBS check, identity and reference checks, and safeguarding training. Schools can request the supporting records.
A page for your school
Your school has its own lesson page showing its name, address and available instruments. Parents use that page to apply.
The running of it
Our team handles attendance records, billing and family enquiries, including requests to change lessons.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
We need a room for teaching and an agreed slot within the school day. We work with you on a rotating timetable.
One person to ask
We ask for one main contact at school to coordinate access, confirm dates and let us know about changes.

When enquiring for a school in Bridgwater, or nearby in Taunton, Bath and Weston-super-Mare, please confirm teaching availability with our team.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Bridgwater depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Prices chosen by the school

    Each school chooses its lesson price. After teacher pay, programme operating costs and job-advertising costs, MML pays 50% of the remaining lesson income directly into the school’s account each month. See school prices and monthly payouts ↗.

  • Family lesson payments

    Parents contract with My Music Lessons and pay us directly at the price their school chooses. See pricing and monthly payouts ↗.
