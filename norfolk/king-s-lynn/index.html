King's Lynn's schools, and the music we run in them

We staff it, cover it and record it. Your school keeps its timetable.

The music in a King's Lynn school week does not have to be an add-on. We staff it, run it and report on it as part of the ordinary day.

Counting the ones just outside it, King's Lynn has 49 primary schools. Fairstead Community Primary and Nursery School is the largest, at around 430 children, and 5 of them are the size an in-school music service usually runs in.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a King's Lynn school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in King's Lynn depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in King's Lynn

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for King's Lynn

What a school is actually buying is the absence of a problem: no cover to find, no instruments to insure, no safeguarding file to keep, no invoices to chase. In King's Lynn that is the whole of our side of it.

Children in King's Lynn learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

In-school music service in King's Lynn

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in King's Lynn.

When a teacher on the King's Lynn round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

School music lessons in King's Lynn

Lessons one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, during the school day, with a practice note going home after each one. That is the shape of it in King's Lynn.

A lesson in King's Lynn is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

Peripatetic music teachers in King's Lynn

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through King's Lynn has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

What a King's Lynn school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Instrumental tuition in King's Lynn

Tuition on the instrument the child actually wants, taught by somebody who plays it, inside the King's Lynn school day.

King's Lynn sits on the same weekly round as Norwich, Great Yarmouth and Thetford, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

Music lessons for King's Lynn schools

Provision usually fails on administration rather than on teaching. A King's Lynn school gives us a room and a slot; we hold the staffing, the cover, the safeguarding evidence and the billing, and hand the registers back.

King's Lynn is in Norfolk, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

School band and ensemble in King's Lynn

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In King's Lynn that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.

In King's Lynn that covers 49 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.
