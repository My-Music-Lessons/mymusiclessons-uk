THE WEEKLY ROUND

How the county is covered

Our teachers travel a fixed weekly round across Herefordshire, and every one of these 6 towns sits on one. That is what makes cover possible: when a teacher is ill, the person who covers the lesson is already working in the next school along, and your office is not asked to find anybody.

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what a Herefordshire school provides

If you want more teaching time later you ask for it, and what was agreed stays exactly as it is while the request is looked at. Nothing moves underneath you.

Ours to provide

The instruments
At no cost to the school, and no stock to buy before you know whether your children want it.
Checks and training

Your own lesson page, with your name, your address and the instruments actually offered. Parents start there.

One person to ask

Herefordshire is part of West Midlands, and nothing in the two lists above changes from one school to the next.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Herefordshire depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

The service across Herefordshire

The questions a county-wide enquiry tends to arrive with, answered once rather than on a page each.

The music service for Herefordshire

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Herefordshire schools.

A lesson anywhere in Herefordshire is one to five children, inside the school day, and it is the same shape in every town on this page.

In-school music service in Herefordshire

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Herefordshire.

Herefordshire is part of West Midlands, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with here.

School music lessons in Herefordshire

A lesson in Herefordshire is one child, or two or three learning the same thing alongside each other, or up to five playing one piece together. Which of those a child gets depends on the child, not on what is cheapest to run.

The rounds through Herefordshire take in Hereford, Leominster and Ross-on-Wye and the rest of the 6 towns listed on this page.

A music partner for Herefordshire schools

A supplier sends invoices and an agenda. A partner takes the part of the job a Herefordshire school does not want - staffing, cover, records, billing - and leaves the school with the part only the school can do.

A Herefordshire school provides a room and a rotating slot, and pays only for the places it chooses to fund. The cover, the instruments and the billing are ours.
