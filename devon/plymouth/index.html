Plymouth schools, and the music service inside them

We staff it, cover it and record it. Your school keeps its timetable.

Our Plymouth teaching sits inside the timetable. A child leaves a lesson, plays for half an hour, and goes back to the next one.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Plymouth school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Plymouth depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Plymouth

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Plymouth

A music service is the whole thing, not a teacher: staffing, cover when somebody is ill, instruments, records of who played what, and one invoice. That is what we run in Plymouth.

Children in Plymouth learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

In-school music service in Plymouth

In school means in the building, in the timetable, in term time. Our teachers hold a room in Plymouth schools and children come to them between lessons.

Plymouth sits on the same weekly round as Exeter, Torquay and Paignton, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

School music lessons in Plymouth

A lesson in Plymouth is one child, or two or three learning the same thing alongside each other, or up to five playing one piece together. Which of those a child gets depends on the child, not on what is cheapest to run.

In Plymouth that covers 75 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Peripatetic music teachers in Plymouth

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Plymouth has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

Plymouth is in Devon, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Instrumental tuition in Plymouth

A child who chose their instrument practises. A child who was handed one usually does not. So tuition in Plymouth starts from what the child asked for, taught by somebody whose own instrument it is.

A lesson in Plymouth is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

Music lessons for Plymouth schools

Provision usually fails on administration rather than on teaching. A Plymouth school gives us a room and a slot; we hold the staffing, the cover, the safeguarding evidence and the billing, and hand the registers back.

The 75 primary schools listed for Plymouth come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

School band and ensemble in Plymouth

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In Plymouth that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.

What a Plymouth school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.
