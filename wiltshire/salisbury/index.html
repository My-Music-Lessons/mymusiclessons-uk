ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Salisbury school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Salisbury depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Salisbury

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Salisbury

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Salisbury schools.

In Salisbury that covers 49 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

In-school music service in Salisbury

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Salisbury.

Salisbury is in Wiltshire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

School music lessons in Salisbury

Lessons one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, during the school day, with a practice note going home after each one. That is the shape of it in Salisbury.

Children in Salisbury learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

Peripatetic music teachers in Salisbury

Our teachers travel to schools across Salisbury on a fixed weekly round, which is what makes cover possible when one of them is ill - the school is never left with an empty room.

What a Salisbury school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Instrumental tuition in Salisbury

Tuition on the instrument the child actually wants, taught by somebody who plays it, inside the Salisbury school day.

The 49 primary schools listed for Salisbury come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

Music lessons for Salisbury schools

Provision usually fails on administration rather than on teaching. A Salisbury school gives us a room and a slot; we hold the staffing, the cover, the safeguarding evidence and the billing, and hand the registers back.

A lesson in Salisbury is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

School band and ensemble in Salisbury

Five is the largest number where everybody still has a part worth playing. A band that size in Salisbury is the point at which a child stops counting bars on their own and starts listening to somebody else.

When a teacher on the Salisbury round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.
