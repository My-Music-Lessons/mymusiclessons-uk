ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Southampton school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what a Southampton school provides

Provision can grow. A school asks for more time, and the arrangement already in place carries on unchanged until a new one is agreed.

Ours to provide

The instruments
A child wanting to try the drums needs no drum kit at home. The instruments cost the school nothing.
Checks and training
Enhanced DBS, identity, references and safeguarding training, done before a first lesson. We hold the evidence and show it on request.
A page for your school
Families apply where they already are, on a page about their own school. Your office is not in the middle of it.
The running of it
Registers, practice notes, invoices and the messages home. A parent with a question asks us, not you.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
A room to teach in and a slot that moves through the week. Nothing else on your timetable changes.
One person to ask
A name we can reach when a room changes. It is rarely more than a message.

In Southampton that is the same arrangement in every school we work with, across all 108 of the primaries listed on this page.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Southampton depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

More about the service in Southampton

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Southampton

A music service is the whole thing, not a teacher: staffing, cover when somebody is ill, instruments, records of who played what, and one invoice. That is what we run in Southampton.

In Southampton that covers 108 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

In-school music service in Southampton

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Southampton.

Children in Southampton learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

School music lessons in Southampton

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Southampton knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

A lesson in Southampton is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

Peripatetic music teachers in Southampton

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Southampton has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

The 108 primary schools listed for Southampton come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

Instrumental tuition in Southampton

A child who chose their instrument practises. A child who was handed one usually does not. So tuition in Southampton starts from what the child asked for, taught by somebody whose own instrument it is.

Southampton sits on the same weekly round as Portsmouth, Basingstoke and Winchester, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

Music lessons for Southampton schools

What we ask of a Southampton school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.

Southampton is in Hampshire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

School band and ensemble in Southampton

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In Southampton that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.

What a Southampton school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.
