My Music Lessons · YOUR FREE SCHOOL MUSIC TOOLS

A useful starting route.

Start with a clear purpose, then check interest, resources, time and costs before bringing a proposal to the school team.

The answers this route uses

Your answers have changed. Build the route again to update these actions and the answer record.

  1. 01

    School music action worksheet ↗

    A first musical purpose gives the team something concrete to plan around before it chooses equipment or a timetable.

    Bring back: Write the first musical aim, one next action, a responsible school role and a review point.

    mymusiclessons.uk/school-music-action-plan/
  2. 02

    Free sign-up poster ↗

    Understanding interest helps the team plan instruments and a realistic first group before it offers places.

    Bring back: Prepare a poster and use the school’s own approved route to gather interest. Keep pupil responses with the school.

    mymusiclessons.uk/student-interest-poster/
  3. 03

    Instrument demand and equipment planner ↗

    Comparing interest with usable equipment makes resource gaps visible before the team assumes every interested pupil can play at once.

    Bring back: Compare aggregate instrument interest with usable equipment. Discuss reuse, repair, loans, hire and phased starts before buying.

    mymusiclessons.uk/school-music-instrument-planner/
  4. 04

    Music club session planner ↗

    A rotation model helps the team see how individual playing time and changeovers fit into the weekly window.

    Bring back: Test a weekly window using available stations, individual playing time and changeovers. Confirm staffing and practical arrangements separately.

    mymusiclessons.uk/music-club-planner/
  5. 05

    School music budget planner ↗

    A full-period estimate brings setup and weekly running costs into the same conversation, including the school’s staff time.

    Bring back: List setup, weekly staff time and running costs on a consistent basis. Check estimates and keep missing quotes open.

    mymusiclessons.uk/music-club-budget/
  6. 06

    School music proposal builder ↗

    A discussion paper can make the lead, supervision arrangements and school approval route explicit before a launch decision.

    Bring back: Bring the purpose, capacity and cost assumptions into a discussion paper. Record the school roles and decisions still needed.

    mymusiclessons.uk/school-music-proposal/

This route is based on staff self-report. It is a planning suggestion, not an assessment, school approval or confirmation of service availability. The school team decides what to do next.

Each tool supports a school discussion. Review tuition, supervision, suitability, school policy and supplier arrangements through your school’s own process. Confirm the service with MML; pupil access is not open yet.
