THE WEEKLY ROUND

How the county is covered

Our teachers travel a fixed weekly round across Suffolk, and every one of these 8 towns sits on one. That is what makes cover possible: when a teacher is ill, the person who covers the lesson is already working in the next school along, and your office is not asked to find anybody.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Suffolk depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

The service across Suffolk

The questions a county-wide enquiry tends to arrive with, answered once rather than on a page each.

The music service for Suffolk

A music service is the whole thing, not a teacher: staffing, cover when somebody is ill, instruments, records of who played what, and one invoice. That is what we run in Suffolk.

Across Suffolk that is 8 towns and cities, and every one of them sits on a weekly round.

In-school music service in Suffolk

In school means in the building, in the timetable, in term time. Our teachers hold a room in Suffolk schools and children come to them between lessons.

A Suffolk school provides a room and a rotating slot, and pays only for the places it chooses to fund. The cover, the instruments and the billing are ours.

School music lessons in Suffolk

A lesson in Suffolk is one child, or two or three learning the same thing alongside each other, or up to five playing one piece together. Which of those a child gets depends on the child, not on what is cheapest to run.

The rounds through Suffolk take in Ipswich, Bury St Edmunds and Lowestoft and the rest of the 8 towns listed on this page.

A music partner for Suffolk schools

A supplier sends invoices and an agenda. A partner takes the part of the job a Suffolk school does not want - staffing, cover, records, billing - and leaves the school with the part only the school can do.

Suffolk is part of East of England, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with here.
