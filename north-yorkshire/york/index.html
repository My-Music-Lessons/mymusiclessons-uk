ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From an enquiry to music at your school in York

  1. 01

    One conversation

    Tell us about your school, existing music provision and what you would like to offer. We discuss suitable options and current availability.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    We agree a suitable teaching space and lesson times with your school. A rotating timetable can help share missed class time across subjects.

  3. 03

    Teaching and absence cover

    We organise teachers and absence cover. Teaching can be one-to-one, in small groups or in bands of up to five, following the programme agreed with your school.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    We maintain registers, practice notes and safeguarding records for your team. Where families pay for places, we handle their lesson billing directly.

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what schools in York provide

Schools can ask to expand their music provision. We assess requests for more time while keeping the agreed lessons in place.

Ours to provide

The instruments
We provide the instruments for lessons at no cost to the school. Children can try the drums without owning a kit at home.
Checks and training
Every teacher has an enhanced DBS check, identity and reference checks, and safeguarding training. Schools can request the supporting records.
A page for your school
We give parents a school-specific page with details of the lessons on offer and an application form.
The running of it
Day-to-day lesson administration is managed by our team. Your school has a named contact for questions or issues that need attention.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
Your school provides a teaching room and time for lessons during term. We agree the timetable together and organise the lesson administration.
One person to ask
We ask for one main contact at school to coordinate access, confirm dates and let us know about changes.

Schools in York can enquire about Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele. We provide lesson instruments at no cost to the school; available teaching options are confirmed during the enquiry.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in York depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Prices chosen by the school

    Each school chooses its lesson price. After teacher pay, programme operating costs and job-advertising costs, MML pays 50% of the remaining lesson income directly into the school’s account each month. See school prices and monthly payouts ↗.

  • Family lesson payments

    Parents contract with My Music Lessons and pay us directly at the price their school chooses. See pricing and monthly payouts ↗.

More about the service in York

Practical answers for school leaders and music leads: what the service includes, how lessons fit into school life and how to discuss provision.

The music service for York

A music service brings teaching, instruments, absence cover and administration into one school agreement. For a school in York, we discuss the proposed lessons, records and billing together so staff know who is responsible for each part.

You can also explore the nearby town pages for Harrogate, Scarborough and Middlesbrough. For an enquiry in York, include the school name and location so we can discuss its own arrangements.

In-school music service in York

Schools in York can discuss a programme that fits their existing timetable. We agree the room, lesson times and how the slots rotate with the school, then organise teaching and instruments for the agreed provision.

The register on this page lists 103 primary schools in York. Use it to find the school you want to ask about; a listing does not confirm that lessons are running there.

School music lessons in York

The lesson format is agreed with the school: individual tuition, a small group or a band of up to five. Schools in York can ask which formats suit their proposed instruments and pupils, and how progress and practice notes are shared with families.

York is in North Yorkshire. When contacting us, include the school name so we can discuss provision for that school.

Peripatetic music teachers in York

Peripatetic teaching allows a school to arrange specialist music lessons on its own premises. Schools in York can ask about teacher availability, safeguarding checks, lesson records and how the programme would fit into the school week.

The 103 primary schools listed for York are drawn from the Department for Education register. The list describes local schools, not current service availability.

Instrumental tuition in York

Tuition includes practical teaching, feedback and a note home explaining what to practise. Schools in York can ask how the teaching is organised for individual pupils and small groups, and how lesson records help families follow their progress.

For a school in York, lessons can be arranged one-to-one, in small groups or in bands of up to five during the school day. The format is agreed with the school.

Music lessons for York schools

A school in York can start with the space and time it has available. We discuss the staffing, instruments and administration needed for the programme, including how families apply and whether places are funded by the school or paid for by families.

A school in York supplies a room and a rotating lesson slot. Each school chooses its lesson price. Parents contract with and pay My Music Lessons directly. After teacher pay, programme operating and job-advertising costs, we pay 50% of the remaining lesson income directly into the school’s account each month.

School band and ensemble in York

Playing in a group involves keeping time with others and learning how individual parts fit together. Schools in York can ask about bands and small ensembles of up to five, including the room, instruments and timetable needed.

For an enquiry about a school in York, tell us the proposed lesson times and room. We will discuss teaching availability and the arrangements for absence cover.
