Bringing a music service to a York school

We staff it, cover it and record it. Your school keeps its timetable.

Parents in York tell us the same thing: the will is there and the week is not. Teaching in school is our answer to that.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a York school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in York depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

In-school music service in York

School music lessons in York

Peripatetic music teachers in York

The word is old and the arrangement is simple: the same teacher comes to your York school every week, knows the children by name, and belongs to a round rather than to a booking.

The 103 primary schools listed for York come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

Instrumental tuition in York

Tuition rather than an activity: there is a thing to learn, a way of telling whether it is being learnt, and a note home saying what to practise before next week. That is what a York family is paying for, or what a York school is funding on their behalf.

A lesson in York is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

Music lessons for York schools

What we ask of a York school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.

What a York school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

School band and ensemble in York

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In York that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.

When a teacher on the York round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.
