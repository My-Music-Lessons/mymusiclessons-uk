WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what schools in Essex provide

Schools can ask to expand their music provision. We assess requests for more time while keeping the agreed lessons in place.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Lesson instruments are free to the school. The teacher brings the equipment and takes it away after teaching.
Checks and training
Teachers complete safeguarding training and enhanced DBS, identity and reference checks before joining the teaching team. We keep the records available for schools to review.
A page for your school
Your school has its own lesson page showing its name, address and available instruments. Parents use that page to apply.
The running of it
Our team handles attendance records, billing and family enquiries, including requests to change lessons.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
Your school provides a teaching room and time for lessons during term. We agree the timetable together and organise the lesson administration.
One person to ask
Choose a school contact who can help with practical questions about rooms, dates and timetable changes.

For schools in Essex, including those near Chelmsford, Colchester and Basildon, teaching availability and scheduling are confirmed with our team.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Essex depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Prices chosen by the school

    Each school chooses its lesson price. After teacher pay, programme operating costs and job-advertising costs, MML pays 50% of the remaining lesson income directly into the school’s account each month. See school prices and monthly payouts ↗.

  • Family lesson payments

    Parents contract with My Music Lessons and pay us directly at the price their school chooses. See pricing and monthly payouts ↗.

The music service for Essex

Setting up music provision involves staffing, a timetable, instruments and communication with families. Schools in Essex can discuss these arrangements with our team, including how we manage registers, practice notes and teacher absence.

This page links to 9 towns and cities in Essex. Each town page has a school register to help you find the school you want to ask about.

In-school music service in Essex

An in-school music service takes place on school premises, during the school day in term time. A school in Essex provides a suitable room and agrees lesson slots with us; children attend their music lesson and then return to class.

The local pages for Essex include Chelmsford, Colchester and Basildon. They are part of the 9 towns listed here, with registers to help schools and families find the right school.

School music lessons in Essex

Schools in Essex can discuss one-to-one lessons, small groups and bands of up to five children. Teaching takes place during the school day, with a practice note sent home after each lesson to help families follow what their child is learning.

Schools in Essex can ask about Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele. The instruments and lesson format are confirmed with each school as part of its agreement.

A music partner for Essex schools

A school partnership sets out what each team provides and how the service will run. Schools in Essex can discuss teaching, absence cover, instruments, records and billing with us, alongside the room and timetable the school supplies.

If your school in Essex is considering a partnership, ask how staffing and absence cover would work for its proposed timetable. Our team will confirm what can be arranged.
