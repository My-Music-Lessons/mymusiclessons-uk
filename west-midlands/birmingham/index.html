Birmingham: a music service inside the school day

We staff it, cover it and record it. Your school keeps its timetable.

We put music into the Birmingham school day rather than on top of it, because that is the version families can actually keep up.

There are 301 primary schools in and around Birmingham. The largest is Ward End Primary School, with around 880 children, and 153 of them are the size an in-school music service usually runs in.

Teachers, instruments and cover

We staff the lessons and cover them when somebody is ill. Your school does not find a replacement.

Inside the school day

A room and a rotating slot. One-to-one, small groups, and bands of up to five, in term time, so the same subject is never missed twice.

Safeguarding and records held by us

Checks, registers, practice notes and evidence, kept by us and available to your team whenever you ask.

Two payers, and never both

A place is funded by the school or paid for by the family, never part of each. Many schools fund a set number of places and the other families pay. Which applies depends on the school, not on who asks first.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Birmingham school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Birmingham depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Birmingham

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Birmingham

What a school is actually buying is the absence of a problem: no cover to find, no instruments to insure, no safeguarding file to keep, no invoices to chase. In Birmingham that is the whole of our side of it.

What a Birmingham school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

In-school music service in Birmingham

In school means in the building, in the timetable, in term time. Our teachers hold a room in Birmingham schools and children come to them between lessons.

Birmingham sits on the same weekly round as coventry, wolverhampton and solihull, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

School music lessons in Birmingham

A lesson in Birmingham is one child, or two or three learning the same thing alongside each other, or up to five playing one piece together. Which of those a child gets depends on the child, not on what is cheapest to run.

Birmingham is in West Midlands, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Peripatetic music teachers in Birmingham

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Birmingham has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

What a Birmingham school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Instrumental tuition in Birmingham

A child who chose their instrument practises. A child who was handed one usually does not. So tuition in Birmingham starts from what the child asked for, taught by somebody whose own instrument it is.

What a Birmingham school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Music lessons for Birmingham schools

What we ask of a Birmingham school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.

Birmingham sits on the same weekly round as coventry, wolverhampton and solihull, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

School band and ensemble in Birmingham

Up to five children, one room, one piece of music. A band or a small ensemble is where a child stops practising and starts playing with other people, which is the part a lesson on its own cannot give them - and in Birmingham it happens inside the school day like everything else we run.

Birmingham is in West Midlands, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.
