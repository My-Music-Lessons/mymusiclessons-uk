WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what schools in Leicester provide

If your school needs more lesson time, we can discuss the options. The current agreement remains in place until a new arrangement is agreed.

Ours to provide

The instruments
We provide the instruments for lessons at no cost to the school. Children can try the drums without owning a kit at home.
Checks and training
Before teaching begins, we complete enhanced DBS, identity and reference checks and safeguarding training. Evidence is available on request.
A page for your school
Your school has its own lesson page showing its name, address and available instruments. Parents use that page to apply.
The running of it
Our team handles attendance records, billing and family enquiries, including requests to change lessons.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
We need a room for teaching and an agreed slot within the school day. We work with you on a rotating timetable.
One person to ask
Choose a school contact who can help with practical questions about rooms, dates and timetable changes.

When enquiring for a school in Leicester, or nearby in Loughborough, Hinckley and Melton Mowbray, please confirm teaching availability with our team.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Leicester depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

The music service for Leicester

Our service includes the teaching and the work around it: arranging teachers and instruments, keeping lesson records, managing billing and organising absence cover. A school in Leicester can ask how each part would work before agreeing a partnership.

In-school music service in Leicester

Schools in Leicester can discuss a programme that fits their existing timetable. We agree the room, lesson times and how the slots rotate with the school, then organise teaching and instruments for the agreed provision.

The instruments schools in Leicester can ask about are Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele. We confirm the programme and availability with each school before lessons are agreed.

School music lessons in Leicester

The lesson format is agreed with the school: individual tuition, a small group or a band of up to five. Schools in Leicester can ask which formats suit their proposed instruments and pupils, and how progress and practice notes are shared with families.

You can also explore the nearby town pages for Loughborough, Hinckley and Melton Mowbray. For an enquiry in Leicester, include the school name and location so we can discuss its own arrangements.

Peripatetic music teachers in Leicester

A visiting music teacher works within the school timetable. For a school in Leicester, we agree the teaching arrangements as part of the service and organise absence cover; staff can ask how missed lessons are handled before signing an agreement.

A school in Leicester supplies a room and a rotating lesson slot. The agreement also confirms any places the school chooses to fund and the arrangements for family-paid places.

Instrumental tuition in Leicester

Tuition includes practical teaching, feedback and a note home explaining what to practise. Schools in Leicester can ask how the teaching is organised for individual pupils and small groups, and how lesson records help families follow their progress.

For an enquiry about a school in Leicester, tell us the proposed lesson times and room. We will discuss teaching availability and the arrangements for absence cover.

Music lessons for Leicester schools

A school in Leicester can start with the space and time it has available. We discuss the staffing, instruments and administration needed for the programme, including how families apply and whether places are funded by the school or paid for by families.

For a school in Leicester, lessons can be arranged one-to-one, in small groups or in bands of up to five during the school day. The format is agreed with the school.

School band and ensemble in Leicester

Playing in a group involves keeping time with others and learning how individual parts fit together. Schools in Leicester can ask about bands and small ensembles of up to five, including the room, instruments and timetable needed.
