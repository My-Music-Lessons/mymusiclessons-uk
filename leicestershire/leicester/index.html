Leicester schools, and the music service inside them

We staff it, cover it and record it. Your school keeps its timetable.

Learning an instrument in Leicester usually founders on the school run. Our service takes that out of it by teaching in the school itself.

There are 153 primary schools in and around Leicester. The largest is Alderman Richard Hallam Primary School, with around 880 children, and 79 of them are the size an in-school music service usually runs in.

Teachers, instruments and cover

We staff the lessons and cover them when somebody is ill. Your school does not find a replacement.

Inside the school day

A room and a rotating slot. One-to-one, small groups, and bands of up to five, in term time, so the same subject is never missed twice.

Safeguarding and records held by us

Checks, registers, practice notes and evidence, kept by us and available to your team whenever you ask.

Two payers, and never both

A place is funded by the school or paid for by the family, never part of each. Many schools fund a set number of places and the other families pay. Which applies depends on the school, not on who asks first.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Leicester school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what a Leicester school provides

Provision can grow. A school asks for more time, and the arrangement already in place carries on unchanged until a new one is agreed.

Ours to provide

The instruments
A child wanting to try the drums needs no drum kit at home. The instruments cost the school nothing.
Checks and training
Enhanced DBS, identity, references and safeguarding training, done before a first lesson. We hold the evidence and show it on request.
A page for your school
Your own lesson page, with your name, your address and the instruments actually offered. Parents start there.
The running of it
Attendance, billing and family contact are ours. One route for a question, one for a change, neither ends with you.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
A room to teach in and a slot that moves through the week. Nothing else on your timetable changes.
One person to ask
Someone who can answer a question about a room or a date. Most weeks there is nothing to ask.

Leicester sits on the round with Loughborough, Hinckley and Melton Mowbray, which is why a missed week here is covered rather than refunded.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Leicester depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Leicester

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Leicester

What a school is actually buying is the absence of a problem: no cover to find, no instruments to insure, no safeguarding file to keep, no invoices to chase. In Leicester that is the whole of our side of it.

In Leicester that covers 153 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

In-school music service in Leicester

Nothing happens after the bell. A child in Leicester walks to the room, has their lesson and walks back, and the only thing that changes on your timetable is that a room is booked.

Children in Leicester learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

School music lessons in Leicester

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Leicester knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

Leicester sits on the same weekly round as Loughborough, Hinckley and Melton Mowbray, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

Peripatetic music teachers in Leicester

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Leicester has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

What a Leicester school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Instrumental tuition in Leicester

Tuition rather than an activity: there is a thing to learn, a way of telling whether it is being learnt, and a note home saying what to practise before next week. That is what a Leicester family is paying for, or what a Leicester school is funding on their behalf.

When a teacher on the Leicester round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

Music lessons for Leicester schools

What we ask of a Leicester school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.

A lesson in Leicester is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

School band and ensemble in Leicester

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In Leicester that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.
