WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what schools in Lincoln provide

Schools can ask to expand their music provision. We assess requests for more time while keeping the agreed lessons in place.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Instruments are supplied at no cost to the school, so you can introduce lessons without buying equipment first.
Checks and training
Every teacher has an enhanced DBS check, identity and reference checks, and safeguarding training. Schools can request the supporting records.
A page for your school
Families apply through a dedicated page for your school, with the lesson information they need to get started.
The running of it
Our team handles attendance records, billing and family enquiries, including requests to change lessons.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
Provide a suitable room and agree a rotating lesson time with us to help children avoid repeatedly missing the same subject.
One person to ask
Choose a school contact who can help with practical questions about rooms, dates and timetable changes.

Schools in Lincoln can enquire about Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele. We provide lesson instruments at no cost to the school; available teaching options are confirmed during the enquiry.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Lincoln depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.
