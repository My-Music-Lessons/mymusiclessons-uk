ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Spalding school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what a Spalding school provides

The agreement is a starting point rather than a ceiling. Ask for more and the existing one holds while we work out what is possible.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Free to the school, brought by the teacher and taken away again. Nothing to buy, nothing to store.
Checks and training
Enhanced DBS, identity, references and safeguarding training, done before a first lesson. We hold the evidence and show it on request.
A page for your school
Your own lesson page, with your name, your address and the instruments actually offered. Parents start there.
The running of it
Attendance, billing and family contact are ours. One route for a question, one for a change, neither ends with you.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
One room and one recurring slot in term time. Everything around it is ours to arrange.
One person to ask
A name we can reach when a room changes. It is rarely more than a message.

In Spalding that is the same arrangement in every school we work with, across all 34 of the primaries listed on this page.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Spalding depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Spalding

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Spalding

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Spalding schools.

Children in Spalding learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

In-school music service in Spalding

In school means in the building, in the timetable, in term time. Our teachers hold a room in Spalding schools and children come to them between lessons.

The 34 primary schools listed for Spalding come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

School music lessons in Spalding

A lesson in Spalding is one child, or two or three learning the same thing alongside each other, or up to five playing one piece together. Which of those a child gets depends on the child, not on what is cheapest to run.

What a Spalding school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Peripatetic music teachers in Spalding

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Spalding has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

A lesson in Spalding is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

Instrumental tuition in Spalding

Tuition rather than an activity: there is a thing to learn, a way of telling whether it is being learnt, and a note home saying what to practise before next week. That is what a Spalding family is paying for, or what a Spalding school is funding on their behalf.

In Spalding that covers 34 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Music lessons for Spalding schools

Provision usually fails on administration rather than on teaching. A Spalding school gives us a room and a slot; we hold the staffing, the cover, the safeguarding evidence and the billing, and hand the registers back.

Spalding sits on the same weekly round as Lincoln, Grimsby and Scunthorpe, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

School band and ensemble in Spalding

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In Spalding that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.

When a teacher on the Spalding round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.
