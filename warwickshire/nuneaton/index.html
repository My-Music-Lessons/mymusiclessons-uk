ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Nuneaton school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Nuneaton depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Nuneaton

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Nuneaton

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Nuneaton schools.

Children in Nuneaton learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

In-school music service in Nuneaton

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Nuneaton.

A lesson in Nuneaton is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

School music lessons in Nuneaton

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Nuneaton knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

What a Nuneaton school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Peripatetic music teachers in Nuneaton

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Nuneaton has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

When a teacher on the Nuneaton round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

Instrumental tuition in Nuneaton

A child who chose their instrument practises. A child who was handed one usually does not. So tuition in Nuneaton starts from what the child asked for, taught by somebody whose own instrument it is.

In Nuneaton that covers 31 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Music lessons for Nuneaton schools

For a school this is provision without administration: we staff it, cover it, record it and bill the families directly. Nuneaton schools keep their timetable and lose the paperwork.

The 31 primary schools listed for Nuneaton come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

School band and ensemble in Nuneaton

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In Nuneaton that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.
