Read the assumptions before using the comparison

Weekly places means places in one assumed weekly delivery window. The first-window gap is planned participants minus weekly places, with a minimum of zero. Repeated weekly windows = participants ÷ weekly places, rounded up; this assumes different participants in each window. It does not prove that an ongoing weekly place can be offered to everyone. Zero weekly places needs a new capacity assumption before a rollout can be modelled.

Capacity stays with its school: spare places at one school do not cancel a gap at another. The worksheet adds known setup costs and known weekly costs separately, counts missing estimates and does not invent a full-period budget.

Explore, Prepare and Review are editable planning labels. This worksheet does not assess suitability, permissions, safety, school access, supported ages or available teaching capacity. Agree the actual arrangements with each school and relevant suppliers before delivery.
