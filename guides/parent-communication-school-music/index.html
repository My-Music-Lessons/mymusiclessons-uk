FREE TO USE · MY MUSIC LESSONS
In this guide
  1. Explain what is proposed.
  2. Make the next step obvious.
  3. Keep families informed through the term.

Explain what is proposed.

Name the instruments under discussion, intended lesson format and practical school arrangements. Distinguish gathering interest from confirming places. Leave dates and costs open until the school and provider have agreed them.

Make the next step obvious.

Give families your school-approved sign-up route, a response date and a staff contact. Explain how you will confirm places and what information they will receive before lessons start. Keep responses in school-approved systems.

Keep families informed through the term.

Agree how the school, teacher and families hear about timetable changes, absences and practice expectations. The free diary helps staff organise sessions; it does not send parent messages or replace a parent portal.

Start with this editable letter.

Dear parents and carers,

We are exploring interest in a possible school music club at [school name]. We would like to understand which instruments pupils are interested in and the practical arrangements families would need.

This is an expression of interest, not an enrolment form or an offer of a confirmed place. The school will provide further information about suitability, tuition, staff supervision, session times, instruments and any charges before families decide whether to take part.

To tell us you are interested, please [school-managed response instructions] by [response date]. Please use the school’s approved response route.

We will review interest alongside the time, equipment and support the school can provide, then explain the next steps.

If you have a question, please contact [school role / usual school contact route].

Thank you,
[school team]
