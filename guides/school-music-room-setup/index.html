Choose a suitable teaching space.

Discuss the room with the teacher. Check visibility and safeguarding arrangements, access, seating, ventilation and the effect of sound on neighbouring classes. A quieter room helps pupils hear instructions and concentrate.

Match the room to the instrument.

Check the piano or keyboard, tuning, instrument sizes, safe cables and any amplification the teacher needs. Consider how drums and singing will carry through the building. Keep routes clear and agree how equipment is stored.

Make the teaching day easy to follow.

Agree room access, arrival arrangements, group labels and changeovers. Put closures and unavailable times in the diary before adding repeating sessions. Give the teacher an approved school contact for timetable questions.

Review before the first lesson.

Walk through the room and timetable with the school lead and teacher. Confirm accessibility, emergency procedures, equipment responsibilities and how changes reach the school. A checklist supports the conversation; it is not a safety assessment.
