Choose what the club is for.

Decide whether pupils are learning an instrument, rehearsing together or preparing a performance. The aim determines the teaching, group size and equipment. Start with one manageable group and a review point.

Check interest before promising places.

Use the free sign-up poster and your school’s own registration process. Ask about instruments and suitable times, then review access, additional support and instrument availability. An expression of interest is not a confirmed place.

Agree teaching and school responsibilities.

Confirm who teaches, who supervises, how pupils reach the room and what happens if a session changes. Agree the school’s safeguarding, collection and emergency arrangements. MML can discuss teacher availability and the lesson format with your school.

Put time, equipment and costs together.

Check a suitable room, instruments, storage and changeovers. Use the session planner for a first capacity estimate and the budget tool for your actual cost assumptions. Review the plan with the teacher; a calculated place count does not establish a suitable teaching group.

Launch clearly and review early.

Tell families how to register, what is provided, what they need to bring and how changes are communicated. After the first few sessions, review attendance, the practical timetable and pupils’ experience with the teacher.
