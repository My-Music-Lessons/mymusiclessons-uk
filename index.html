FOR SCHOOLS IN ENGLAND

Music, inside the school day.

We bring the teachers, the instruments and the admin. The school brings a room and a timetable.

My Music Lessons runs instrumental teaching in primary and secondary schools. Children learn in small groups during the school day, on instruments we supply, taught by teachers we recruit, check and pay. Nothing about it lands on your staff.

Talk to us about your schoolHow a school partnership works

Teachers we carry, not you

Recruitment, checks, cover, timetabling and pay are ours. A teacher who cannot come is replaced by us, and the school hears about it from us rather than finding out at the door.

Instruments on the premises

Children learn on instruments we supply and maintain, so a family with nothing at home is not a family whose child cannot start.

Three ways a place is paid for

Some schools fund every place. Some fund a set number and other families pay for theirs. At some schools every place is family-paid. Which applies depends on the school and its funding, not on who asks first.

WHERE WE WORK

Town by town, not everywhere at once

We build a round of schools in one area before opening the next one, because a teacher who is not driving between counties is a teacher who turns up. Our location pages list the towns and counties we are building rounds in, and the schools in each that qualify for us to get in touch.

ONE CONVERSATION

One conversation is enough to know.

Tell us the school and the town. We will say what we could run, when a round reaches you, and which of the three ways a place is paid for would apply.
