ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Stoke-on-Trent school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

What we bring, and what a Stoke-on-Trent school provides

If you want more teaching time later you ask for it, and what was agreed stays exactly as it is while the request is looked at. Nothing moves underneath you.

Free to the school, brought by the teacher and taken away again. Nothing to buy, nothing to store.
Checks and training
Enhanced DBS, identity, references and safeguarding training, done before a first lesson. We hold the evidence and show it on request.

The running of it
Attendance, billing and family contact are ours. One route for a question, one for a change, neither ends with you.

A room and a slot

One person to ask

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Stoke-on-Trent depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Stoke-on-Trent

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Stoke-on-Trent

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Stoke-on-Trent schools.

In Stoke-on-Trent that covers 121 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

In-school music service in Stoke-on-Trent

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Stoke-on-Trent.

What a Stoke-on-Trent school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

School music lessons in Stoke-on-Trent

Lessons one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, during the school day, with a practice note going home after each one. That is the shape of it in Stoke-on-Trent.

A lesson in Stoke-on-Trent is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

Peripatetic music teachers in Stoke-on-Trent

The word is old and the arrangement is simple: the same teacher comes to your Stoke-on-Trent school every week, knows the children by name, and belongs to a round rather than to a booking.

The 121 primary schools listed for Stoke-on-Trent come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

Instrumental tuition in Stoke-on-Trent

Tuition rather than an activity: there is a thing to learn, a way of telling whether it is being learnt, and a note home saying what to practise before next week. That is what a Stoke-on-Trent family is paying for, or what a Stoke-on-Trent school is funding on their behalf.

Stoke-on-Trent is in Staffordshire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Music lessons for Stoke-on-Trent schools

What we ask of a Stoke-on-Trent school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.

When a teacher on the Stoke-on-Trent round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

School band and ensemble in Stoke-on-Trent

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In Stoke-on-Trent that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.

Children in Stoke-on-Trent learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.
