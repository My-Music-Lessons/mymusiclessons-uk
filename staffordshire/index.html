THE WEEKLY ROUND

How the county is covered

Our teachers travel a fixed weekly round across Staffordshire, and every one of these 8 towns sits on one. That is what makes cover possible: when a teacher is ill, the person who covers the lesson is already working in the next school along, and your office is not asked to find anybody.

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what a Staffordshire school provides

Provision can grow. A school asks for more time, and the arrangement already in place carries on unchanged until a new one is agreed.

Ours to provide

The instruments
A child wanting to try the drums needs no drum kit at home. The instruments cost the school nothing.
Checks and training
Enhanced DBS, identity and references on every teacher, safeguarding trained. The file is ours, and yours to see.
A page for your school
Families apply where they already are, on a page about their own school. Your office is not in the middle of it.
The running of it
Registers, practice notes, invoices and the messages home. A parent with a question asks us, not you.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
One room and a time that rotates, so the same child never misses the same subject twice.
One person to ask
A name we can reach when a room changes. It is rarely more than a message.

A school anywhere in Staffordshire that wants the evidence behind any line of this can ask for it. It exists already rather than being assembled when somebody asks.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Staffordshire depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

The service across Staffordshire

The questions a county-wide enquiry tends to arrive with, answered once rather than on a page each.

The music service for Staffordshire

What a school is actually buying is the absence of a problem: no cover to find, no instruments to insure, no safeguarding file to keep, no invoices to chase. In Staffordshire that is the whole of our side of it.

The rounds through Staffordshire take in Stoke-on-Trent, Stafford and Burton upon Trent and the rest of the 8 towns listed on this page.

In-school music service in Staffordshire

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Staffordshire.

Cover across Staffordshire works because the person who covers a missed week is already working in the next school along that round.

School music lessons in Staffordshire

A lesson in Staffordshire is one child, or two or three learning the same thing alongside each other, or up to five playing one piece together. Which of those a child gets depends on the child, not on what is cheapest to run.

Across Staffordshire that is 8 towns and cities, and every one of them sits on a weekly round.

A music partner for Staffordshire schools

A partner rather than a supplier: we hold the room time, the staffing and the safeguarding, and the school holds the relationship with its families. That is how it works across Staffordshire.

Staffordshire is part of West Midlands, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with here.
