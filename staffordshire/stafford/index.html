ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Stafford school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Stafford depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

More about the service in Stafford

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Stafford

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Stafford schools.

Stafford sits on the same weekly round as Stoke-on-Trent, Burton upon Trent and Tamworth, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

In-school music service in Stafford

Nothing happens after the bell. A child in Stafford walks to the room, has their lesson and walks back, and the only thing that changes on your timetable is that a room is booked.

What a Stafford school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

School music lessons in Stafford

Lessons one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, during the school day, with a practice note going home after each one. That is the shape of it in Stafford.

A lesson in Stafford is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

Peripatetic music teachers in Stafford

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Stafford has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

The 45 primary schools listed for Stafford come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

Instrumental tuition in Stafford

Tuition rather than an activity: there is a thing to learn, a way of telling whether it is being learnt, and a note home saying what to practise before next week. That is what a Stafford family is paying for, or what a Stafford school is funding on their behalf.

Children in Stafford learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

Music lessons for Stafford schools

For a school this is provision without administration: we staff it, cover it, record it and bill the families directly. Stafford schools keep their timetable and lose the paperwork.

When a teacher on the Stafford round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

School band and ensemble in Stafford

Up to five children, one room, one piece of music. A band or a small ensemble is where a child stops practising and starts playing with other people, which is the part a lesson on its own cannot give them - and in Stafford it happens inside the school day like everything else we run.

Stafford is in Staffordshire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.
