ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Preston school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

If you want more teaching time later you ask for it, and what was agreed stays exactly as it is while the request is looked at. Nothing moves underneath you.

Registers, practice notes, invoices and the messages home. A parent with a question asks us, not you.

Yours to provide

One person to ask
A name we can reach when a room changes. It is rarely more than a message.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Preston depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Preston

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Preston

What a school is actually buying is the absence of a problem: no cover to find, no instruments to insure, no safeguarding file to keep, no invoices to chase. In Preston that is the whole of our side of it.

Preston sits on the same weekly round as Blackpool, Blackburn and Burnley, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

In-school music service in Preston

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Preston.

The 123 primary schools listed for Preston come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

School music lessons in Preston

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Preston knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

In Preston that covers 123 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Peripatetic music teachers in Preston

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Preston has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

Children in Preston learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

Instrumental tuition in Preston

Tuition on the instrument the child actually wants, taught by somebody who plays it, inside the Preston school day.

Preston is in Lancashire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Music lessons for Preston schools

For a school this is provision without administration: we staff it, cover it, record it and bill the families directly. Preston schools keep their timetable and lose the paperwork.

What a Preston school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

School band and ensemble in Preston

Five is the largest number where everybody still has a part worth playing. A band that size in Preston is the point at which a child stops counting bars on their own and starts listening to somebody else.

When a teacher on the Preston round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.
