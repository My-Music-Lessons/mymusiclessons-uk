Teachers, instruments and cover

We staff the lessons and cover them when somebody is ill. Your school does not find a replacement.

Inside the school day

A room and a rotating slot. One-to-one, small groups, and bands of up to five, in term time, so the same subject is never missed twice.

Safeguarding and records held by us

Checks, registers, practice notes and evidence, kept by us and available to your team whenever you ask.

Two payers, and never both

A place is funded by the school or paid for by the family, never part of each. Many schools fund a set number of places and the other families pay. Which applies depends on the school, not on who asks first.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Manchester depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

ONE CONVERSATION

Tell us about your Manchester school

Name the school and we will find out what is possible there. It costs you a sentence, and it is how most of our partnerships began.

What it costs you

A sentence.

Name the school and we do the finding out - what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what could be on the timetable next term. You get an answer either way, including when the answer is that we are not the right fit for you.

What happens next

  • We reply to the school address you give us, from a person.
  • One short conversation about the room, the slot and who is paying for the places.
  • If it fits, lessons start at the beginning of a term rather than the middle of one.
