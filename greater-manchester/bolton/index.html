WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what schools in Bolton provide

If your school needs more lesson time, we can discuss the options. The current agreement remains in place until a new arrangement is agreed.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Instruments are supplied at no cost to the school, so you can introduce lessons without buying equipment first.
Checks and training
Every teacher has an enhanced DBS check, identity and reference checks, and safeguarding training. Schools can request the supporting records.
A page for your school
We give parents a school-specific page with details of the lessons on offer and an application form.
The running of it
Day-to-day lesson administration is managed by our team. Your school has a named contact for questions or issues that need attention.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
Provide a suitable room and agree a rotating lesson time with us to help children avoid repeatedly missing the same subject.
One person to ask
Choose a school contact who can help with practical questions about rooms, dates and timetable changes.

School leaders in Bolton can request teacher check records and discuss how the partnership would work before making a decision.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Bolton depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

Peripatetic music teachers in Bolton
