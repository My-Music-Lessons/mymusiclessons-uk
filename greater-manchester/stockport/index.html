  1. 01

    One conversation

    Tell us about your school, existing music provision and what you would like to offer. We discuss suitable options and current availability.

What we bring, and what schools in Stockport provide

You can request more teaching time as interest grows. Your existing arrangement continues while we review what can be added.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Instruments are supplied at no cost to the school, so you can introduce lessons without buying equipment first.
Checks and training
Before teaching begins, we complete enhanced DBS, identity and reference checks and safeguarding training. Evidence is available on request.
A page for your school
Your school has its own lesson page showing its name, address and available instruments. Parents use that page to apply.
The running of it
Day-to-day lesson administration is managed by our team. Your school has a named contact for questions or issues that need attention.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
We need a room for teaching and an agreed slot within the school day. We work with you on a rotating timetable.
One person to ask
We ask for one main contact at school to coordinate access, confirm dates and let us know about changes.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Stockport depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.
