Ours to provide

One person to ask
We ask for one main contact at school to coordinate access, confirm dates and let us know about changes.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Rochdale depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

Peripatetic music teachers in Rochdale

Peripatetic teaching allows a school to arrange specialist music lessons on its own premises. Schools in Rochdale can ask about teacher availability, safeguarding checks, lesson records and how the programme would fit into the school week.
