Your Tameside school's music provision, run for you

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Tameside school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Tameside depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Tameside

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Tameside

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Tameside schools.

In Tameside that covers 76 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

In-school music service in Tameside

Nothing happens after the bell. A child in Tameside walks to the room, has their lesson and walks back, and the only thing that changes on your timetable is that a room is booked.

A lesson in Tameside is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

School music lessons in Tameside

A lesson in Tameside is one child, or two or three learning the same thing alongside each other, or up to five playing one piece together. Which of those a child gets depends on the child, not on what is cheapest to run.

Children in Tameside learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

Peripatetic music teachers in Tameside

Our teachers travel to schools across Tameside on a fixed weekly round, which is what makes cover possible when one of them is ill - the school is never left with an empty room.

The 76 primary schools listed for Tameside come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

Instrumental tuition in Tameside

Tuition rather than an activity: there is a thing to learn, a way of telling whether it is being learnt, and a note home saying what to practise before next week. That is what a Tameside family is paying for, or what a Tameside school is funding on their behalf.

What a Tameside school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Music lessons for Tameside schools

What we ask of a Tameside school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.

When a teacher on the Tameside round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

School band and ensemble in Tameside

Five is the largest number where everybody still has a part worth playing. A band that size in Tameside is the point at which a child stops counting bars on their own and starts listening to somebody else.

Tameside sits on the same weekly round as Manchester, Salford and Bolton, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.
