What we bring, and what schools in Tewkesbury provide

Ours to provide

The instruments
Lesson instruments are free to the school. The teacher brings the equipment and takes it away after teaching.

Every teacher has an enhanced DBS check, identity and reference checks, and safeguarding training. Schools can request the supporting records.
A page for your school

Day-to-day lesson administration is managed by our team. Your school has a named contact for questions or issues that need attention.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
We need a room for teaching and an agreed slot within the school day. We work with you on a rotating timetable.
One person to ask
We ask for one main contact at school to coordinate access, confirm dates and let us know about changes.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Tewkesbury depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.
