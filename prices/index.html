HOW BILLING WORKS

A simple, transparent model.

The school chooses its lesson price. MML collects payment, manages programme costs and pays the school's share directly into its account every month.

  1. 01

    The school sets the price

    Your school chooses what families pay for lessons. Families are told the price before they book.

  2. 02

    Parents pay MML

    Parents contract with My Music Lessons and pay us directly. We manage billing and teacher payments.

  3. 03

    The school is paid monthly

    After teacher pay, programme operating costs and job-advertising costs, the school receives 50% of the lesson income remaining. MML pays this share directly into the school's account each month.

THE SCHOOL’S SHARE

Half of what remains goes back to your school.

MML deducts the costs of running the programme before calculating the school’s share. We pay 50% of the remaining lesson income directly into the school's account each month.
