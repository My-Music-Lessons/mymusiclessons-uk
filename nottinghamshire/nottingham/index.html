ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Nottingham school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Nottingham depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Nottingham

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Nottingham

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Nottingham schools.

Nottingham sits on the same weekly round as Mansfield, Worksop and Newark-on-Trent, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

In-school music service in Nottingham

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Nottingham.

Nottingham is in Nottinghamshire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

School music lessons in Nottingham

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Nottingham knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

When a teacher on the Nottingham round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

Peripatetic music teachers in Nottingham

The word is old and the arrangement is simple: the same teacher comes to your Nottingham school every week, knows the children by name, and belongs to a round rather than to a booking.

The 225 primary schools listed for Nottingham come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

Instrumental tuition in Nottingham

Tuition rather than an activity: there is a thing to learn, a way of telling whether it is being learnt, and a note home saying what to practise before next week. That is what a Nottingham family is paying for, or what a Nottingham school is funding on their behalf.

Children in Nottingham learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

Music lessons for Nottingham schools

For a school this is provision without administration: we staff it, cover it, record it and bill the families directly. Nottingham schools keep their timetable and lose the paperwork.

In Nottingham that covers 225 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

School band and ensemble in Nottingham

Five is the largest number where everybody still has a part worth playing. A band that size in Nottingham is the point at which a child stops counting bars on their own and starts listening to somebody else.

A lesson in Nottingham is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.
