ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From an enquiry to music at your school in Nottingham

  1. 01

    One conversation

    Tell us about your school, existing music provision and what you would like to offer. We discuss suitable options and current availability.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    We agree a suitable teaching space and lesson times with your school. A rotating timetable can help share missed class time across subjects.

  3. 03

    Teaching and absence cover

    We organise teachers and absence cover. Teaching can be one-to-one, in small groups or in bands of up to five, following the programme agreed with your school.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    We maintain registers, practice notes and safeguarding records for your team. Where families pay for places, we handle their lesson billing directly.

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what schools in Nottingham provide

You can request more teaching time as interest grows. Your existing arrangement continues while we review what can be added.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Instruments are supplied at no cost to the school, so you can introduce lessons without buying equipment first.
Checks and training
Every teacher has an enhanced DBS check, identity and reference checks, and safeguarding training. Schools can request the supporting records.
A page for your school
We give parents a school-specific page with details of the lessons on offer and an application form.
The running of it
Day-to-day lesson administration is managed by our team. Your school has a named contact for questions or issues that need attention.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
Your school provides a teaching room and time for lessons during term. We agree the timetable together and organise the lesson administration.
One person to ask
A named member of staff helps us keep lessons organised when a room, school event or teaching date changes.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Nottingham depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Prices chosen by the school

    Each school chooses its lesson price. After teacher pay, programme operating costs and job-advertising costs, MML pays 50% of the remaining lesson income directly into the school’s account each month. See school prices and monthly payouts ↗.

  • Family lesson payments

    Parents contract with My Music Lessons and pay us directly at the price their school chooses. See pricing and monthly payouts ↗.
