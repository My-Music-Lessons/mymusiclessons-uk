WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what schools in Worksop provide

You can request more teaching time as interest grows. Your existing arrangement continues while we review what can be added.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Lesson instruments are free to the school. The teacher brings the equipment and takes it away after teaching.
Checks and training
Teachers complete safeguarding training and enhanced DBS, identity and reference checks before joining the teaching team. We keep the records available for schools to review.
A page for your school
Families apply through a dedicated page for your school, with the lesson information they need to get started.
The running of it
Our team handles attendance records, billing and family enquiries, including requests to change lessons.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
Provide a suitable room and agree a rotating lesson time with us to help children avoid repeatedly missing the same subject.
One person to ask
We ask for one main contact at school to coordinate access, confirm dates and let us know about changes.

School leaders in Worksop can request teacher check records and discuss how the partnership would work before making a decision.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Worksop depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Prices chosen by the school

    Each school chooses its lesson price. After teacher pay, programme operating costs and job-advertising costs, MML pays 50% of the remaining lesson income directly into the school’s account each month. See school prices and monthly payouts ↗.

  • Family lesson payments

    Parents contract with My Music Lessons and pay us directly at the price their school chooses. See pricing and monthly payouts ↗.

School music lessons in Worksop
