ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From an enquiry to music at your school in Mansfield

  1. 01

    One conversation

    Tell us about your school, existing music provision and what you would like to offer. We discuss suitable options and current availability.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    We agree a suitable teaching space and lesson times with your school. A rotating timetable can help share missed class time across subjects.

  3. 03

    Teaching and absence cover

    We organise teachers and absence cover. Teaching can be one-to-one, in small groups or in bands of up to five, following the programme agreed with your school.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    We maintain registers, practice notes and safeguarding records for your team. Where families pay for places, we handle their lesson billing directly.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Mansfield depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.
