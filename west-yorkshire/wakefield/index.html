What we bring, and what schools in Wakefield provide

You can request more teaching time as interest grows. Your existing arrangement continues while we review what can be added.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Lesson instruments are free to the school. The teacher brings the equipment and takes it away after teaching.
Checks and training
Teachers complete safeguarding training and enhanced DBS, identity and reference checks before joining the teaching team. We keep the records available for schools to review.
A page for your school
Your school has its own lesson page showing its name, address and available instruments. Parents use that page to apply.
The running of it
Day-to-day lesson administration is managed by our team. Your school has a named contact for questions or issues that need attention.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
Your school provides a teaching room and time for lessons during term. We agree the timetable together and organise the lesson administration.
One person to ask
Choose a school contact who can help with practical questions about rooms, dates and timetable changes.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Wakefield depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

In-school music service in Wakefield

An in-school music service takes place on school premises, during the school day in term time. A school in Wakefield provides a suitable room and agrees lesson slots with us; children attend their music lesson and then return to class.

For a school in Wakefield, lessons can be arranged one-to-one, in small groups or in bands of up to five during the school day. The format is agreed with the school.

School music lessons in Wakefield

A school music programme can include individual teaching and opportunities to play with others. For schools in Wakefield, we discuss the group size, instrument and timetable together, with a maximum of five children in a lesson.

A school in Wakefield supplies a room and a rotating lesson slot. The agreement also confirms any places the school chooses to fund and the arrangements for family-paid places.

Peripatetic music teachers in Wakefield

A visiting music teacher works within the school timetable. For a school in Wakefield, we agree the teaching arrangements as part of the service and organise absence cover; staff can ask how missed lessons are handled before signing an agreement.
