ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Leeds school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Leeds depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

More about the service in Leeds

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Leeds

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Leeds schools.

Leeds is in West Yorkshire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

In-school music service in Leeds

Nothing happens after the bell. A child in Leeds walks to the room, has their lesson and walks back, and the only thing that changes on your timetable is that a room is booked.

What a Leeds school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

School music lessons in Leeds

A lesson in Leeds is one child, or two or three learning the same thing alongside each other, or up to five playing one piece together. Which of those a child gets depends on the child, not on what is cheapest to run.

Leeds is in West Yorkshire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Peripatetic music teachers in Leeds

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Leeds has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

Children here learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

Instrumental tuition in Leeds

A child who chose their instrument practises. A child who was handed one usually does not. So tuition in Leeds starts from what the child asked for, taught by somebody whose own instrument it is.

In Leeds that covers 187 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Music lessons for Leeds schools

For a school this is provision without administration: we staff it, cover it, record it and bill the families directly. Leeds schools keep their timetable and lose the paperwork.

What a Leeds school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

School band and ensemble in Leeds

Five is the largest number where everybody still has a part worth playing. A band that size in Leeds is the point at which a child stops counting bars on their own and starts listening to somebody else.
