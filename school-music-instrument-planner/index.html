How the model works

Feasible places = the smaller of interest and available equipment × chosen rotations. Unmet interest = interest minus feasible places. Extra equipment = interest ÷ rotations, rounded up, minus available equipment, with a minimum of zero.

Repeated windows = interest ÷ the existing equipment capacity per window, rounded up. This assumes different pupils in each repeated window and the same usable equipment each time. Zero equipment cannot be solved by repeating windows. The model does not allocate individual pupils, decide fairness, or test session length, suitability, staffing, acoustics or platform concurrency.

To close a gap, discuss borrowing, hiring, repairing, reusing or buying suitable equipment, or phasing opportunities across repeated windows. Check availability, condition, agreements and actual quotes locally; none of these routes is guaranteed or assumed free.
