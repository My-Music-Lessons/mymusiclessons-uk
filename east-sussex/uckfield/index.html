Uckfield: a music service inside the school day

We staff it, cover it and record it. Your school keeps its timetable.

Every Uckfield school we work with started the same way: children who wanted to play, and a timetable with no room in it. We put the lesson into the school day instead.

13 primary schools teach in and around Uckfield. The largest, Manor Primary School, has around 400 children, and one of them is the size an in-school music service usually runs in.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Uckfield school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Uckfield depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Uckfield

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Uckfield

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Uckfield schools.

Uckfield sits on the same weekly round as Brighton, Hove and Eastbourne, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

In-school music service in Uckfield

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Uckfield.

What a Uckfield school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

School music lessons in Uckfield

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Uckfield knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

Uckfield is in East Sussex, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Peripatetic music teachers in Uckfield

Our teachers travel to schools across Uckfield on a fixed weekly round, which is what makes cover possible when one of them is ill - the school is never left with an empty room.

Children in Uckfield learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

Instrumental tuition in Uckfield

Tuition on the instrument the child actually wants, taught by somebody who plays it, inside the Uckfield school day.

The 13 primary schools listed for Uckfield come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

Music lessons for Uckfield schools

What we ask of a Uckfield school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.

In Uckfield that covers 13 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

School band and ensemble in Uckfield

A child who only ever plays alone gets good and then stops. Put four others in the room and the reason to keep going changes. In Uckfield that happens in the school day, on the same slot as everything else.

When a teacher on the Uckfield round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.
