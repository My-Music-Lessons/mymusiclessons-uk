A LITTLE EXTRA FOR YOUR SCHOOL

Get the whole school talking.

A free, reusable poster. Add your school details, choose the instruments and print. Your school collects interest through its own staff or sign-up page.

01 / Make it your school’s.

Use a sign-up page your school manages. Check its permissions and scan the printed code before putting the poster up.

Use a complete HTTPS link to your school’s sign-up page. A QR code has not been added.

02 / Choose the line-up.

Only show the instruments you are considering.

03 / Choose your print style.

A4 portrait. For A3, enlarge the saved PDF to fit A3 paper. The ink saver uses a white background.

In the print window, choose Save as PDF for a digital copy. Your edits stay on this page and disappear when you reload.

For school staff to prepare.

This poster gathers interest before lesson places are confirmed. No pupil details are collected here. Keep registrations with your school. Agree lessons and practical arrangements before offering places.
