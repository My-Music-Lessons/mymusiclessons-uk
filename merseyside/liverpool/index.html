Liverpool

Merseyside · L1

Your Liverpool school's music provision, run for you

We staff it, cover it and record it. Your school keeps its timetable.

Our work in Liverpool is simple to describe: teachers in school buildings, children in groups of one to five, notes home afterwards.

There are 186 primary schools in and around Liverpool. The largest is Dovedale Community Primary School, with around 840 children, and 77 of them are the size an in-school music service usually runs in.

Teachers, instruments and cover

We staff the lessons and cover them when somebody is ill. Your school does not find a replacement.

Inside the school day

A room and a rotating slot. One-to-one, small groups, and bands of up to five, in term time, so the same subject is never missed twice.

Safeguarding and records held by us

Checks, registers, practice notes and evidence, kept by us and available to your team whenever you ask.

Two payers, and never both

A place is funded by the school or paid for by the family, never part of each. Many schools fund a set number of places and the other families pay. Which applies depends on the school, not on who asks first.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Liverpool school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what a Liverpool school provides

The agreement is a starting point rather than a ceiling. Ask for more and the existing one holds while we work out what is possible.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Free to the school, brought by the teacher and taken away again. Nothing to buy, nothing to store.
Checks and training
Every teacher is DBS checked to enhanced level and safeguarding trained before they join a round, not after.
A page for your school
Families apply where they already are, on a page about their own school. Your office is not in the middle of it.
The running of it
Registers, practice notes, invoices and the messages home. A parent with a question asks us, not you.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
One room and a time that rotates, so the same child never misses the same subject twice.
One person to ask
A name we can reach when a room changes. It is rarely more than a message.

In Liverpool that is the same arrangement in every school we work with, across all 186 of the primaries listed on this page.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Liverpool depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Liverpool

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Liverpool

A music service is the whole thing, not a teacher: staffing, cover when somebody is ill, instruments, records of who played what, and one invoice. That is what we run in Liverpool.

In Liverpool that covers 186 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

In-school music service in Liverpool

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Liverpool.

The 186 primary schools listed for Liverpool come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

School music lessons in Liverpool

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Liverpool knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

A lesson in Liverpool is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

Peripatetic music teachers in Liverpool

Our teachers travel to schools across Liverpool on a fixed weekly round, which is what makes cover possible when one of them is ill - the school is never left with an empty room.

Liverpool is in Merseyside, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Instrumental tuition in Liverpool

Tuition rather than an activity: there is a thing to learn, a way of telling whether it is being learnt, and a note home saying what to practise before next week. That is what a Liverpool family is paying for, or what a Liverpool school is funding on their behalf.

What a Liverpool school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Music lessons for Liverpool schools

Provision usually fails on administration rather than on teaching. A Liverpool school gives us a room and a slot; we hold the staffing, the cover, the safeguarding evidence and the billing, and hand the registers back.

Liverpool sits on the same weekly round as St Helens, Birkenhead and Southport, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

School band and ensemble in Liverpool

Five is the largest number where everybody still has a part worth playing. A band that size in Liverpool is the point at which a child stops counting bars on their own and starts listening to somebody else.

Children in Liverpool learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.
