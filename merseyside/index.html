THE WEEKLY ROUND

How the county is covered

Our teachers travel a fixed weekly round across Merseyside, and every one of these 8 towns sits on one. That is what makes cover possible: when a teacher is ill, the person who covers the lesson is already working in the next school along, and your office is not asked to find anybody.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Merseyside depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

The service across Merseyside

The questions a county-wide enquiry tends to arrive with, answered once rather than on a page each.

The music service for Merseyside

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Merseyside schools.

A Merseyside school provides a room and a rotating slot, and pays only for the places it chooses to fund. The cover, the instruments and the billing are ours.

In-school music service in Merseyside

In school means in the building, in the timetable, in term time. Our teachers hold a room in Merseyside schools and children come to them between lessons.

Across Merseyside that is 8 towns and cities, and every one of them sits on a weekly round.

School music lessons in Merseyside

A lesson in Merseyside is one child, or two or three learning the same thing alongside each other, or up to five playing one piece together. Which of those a child gets depends on the child, not on what is cheapest to run.

A Merseyside school provides a room and a rotating slot, and pays only for the places it chooses to fund. The cover, the instruments and the billing are ours.

A music partner for Merseyside schools

A supplier sends invoices and an agenda. A partner takes the part of the job a Merseyside school does not want - staffing, cover, records, billing - and leaves the school with the part only the school can do.

Merseyside is part of North West, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with here.
